ACME Corp Q2 Earnings Highlights

Source: ACME IR | Date: 2026-02-15

ACME Corp reported Q2 revenue of $2.4B (+8% y/y) and EPS of $1.35, above prior guidance of $1.20–$1.30. Management raised FY revenue guidance to $9.6–$9.8B (from $9.2–$9.6B) on continued strength in enterprise demand.
